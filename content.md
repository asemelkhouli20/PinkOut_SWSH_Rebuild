# PinkOut — Website content

## Positioning
Women-only real-life experiences and events. First gathering planned for Riyadh, with date, venue, programme and prices unannounced. The static site invites exploration and reviews interest; submissions are not enabled by default. No past attendees, partners, statistics or booking availability are invented.

## Narrative
Opening invitation → beginning/results → photographic mood selector → first gathering → complete visible world catalogue → wider community vision → three experience stories → future ideas → practical FAQ → closing invitation. Arabic is primary, English is fully selectable. Voice: direct, relaxed, contemporary Saudi Arabic.
